## Summary

Uses polynomial methods to bound quantum query complexity of Boolean functions, distinguishing total functions from promise problems. The computational resource is oracle queries. This is background on lower-bound arguments, not an FSM-to-network construction or a bound on neural-network inference time. [Evidence](../../raw/quantum-lower-bounds/document.txt).

## Relevance and next question

Identify an explicit reduction and matching resource model before using a quantum query lower bound to support a claim about neural computation.
