## Summary

The abstract establishes worst-case hardness for fitting depth-two ReLU networks under squared loss, including a single-ReLU case. Further lower bounds depend on specified hypotheses; proper and improper learning differ. These training problems have a different input model from compiling a known transition table. [Evidence](../../raw/relu-training-hardness/document.txt).
