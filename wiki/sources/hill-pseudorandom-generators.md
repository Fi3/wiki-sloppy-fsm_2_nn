## Summary

Shows how to construct a pseudorandom generator from any one-way function, linking the existence of the two primitives. It is cryptographic background about efficient computation and computational indistinguishability. It does not give an FSM-to-neural-network conversion procedure. [Evidence](../../raw/hill-pseudorandom-generators/document.txt).
