## Summary

The proceedings abstract reports NP-hardness of minimizing general multi-output Boolean circuits under polynomial-time randomized many-one reductions. Both the multi-output setting and the reduction type matter. This gives relevant context for optimizing a joint next-state/output function. [Evidence](../../raw/multi-output-circuit-minimization/document.txt).

## Relevance and next question

Read the full paper before transferring the result to single-output circuits, a restricted gate library or a particular neural-network optimization problem.
