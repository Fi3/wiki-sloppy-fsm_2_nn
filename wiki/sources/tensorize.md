## Summary

The university abstract describes lifting legacy loop code into tensor DSLs through symbolic tracing, sketches and algebraic solving. It reports correctness by construction and improved synthesis performance on studied benchmarks. The supplied PDF was unavailable, so detailed applicability limits are not established here.
